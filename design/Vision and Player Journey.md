# Vision and Player Journey

**Direction:** Factorio: Space Age is required. This is a multiplayer economic world with public institutions and independent corporations. No hand crafting; factories manufacture all crafted goods. Manual mining and repair rules remain separate questions.

Players arrive at a large walkable central trade station. They find government jobs or corporate employment, take government transport to an assigned site, earn income, and eventually establish businesses. Nauvis is one destination rather than the mandatory starting world.

Proposed journey:

1. Arrive and choose a funded public assignment.
2. Receive issued equipment and transport to the site.
3. Build or operate government infrastructure and earn wages/rewards.
4. Establish a corporation and acquire an industry contract plus a destination/site agreement.
5. Produce goods, hire employees, trade, and fund R&D.
6. Diversify or specialize as planetary infrastructure expands.

Players can remain employees. Government should offer a recovery path without replacing private businesses. Public work develops real public infrastructure and inventories; private businesses supply capacity, specialist equipment, science, and transport.

The central design test: does one company's activity create worthwhile work for another?
