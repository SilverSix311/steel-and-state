# Corporations and Employment

**Direction:** corporations have names, factions, starter equipment, separate research, and employees paid for time worked. They may add industry contracts under one umbrella company.

Proposed records distinguish personal wallet, corporate treasury, owner/member roles, employment agreement, and current shift. Owning a company is independent of working for another company.

A corporation-per-Factorio-force model is a technical candidate, not a tested access-control solution. Temporary employer-force assignment may support shifts; test crafting restrictions, inventory, bonuses, logistics, vehicles, reconnects, and restoring prior membership. Force friendship alone is not a permission system.

Proposed payroll: one active shift, paid online game time, inactivity grace period, and reserved employer funds. Planning and supervising automation count as legitimate work. Disconnection stops new accrual; unpaid time must not be silently promised.

Possible industries: mining/metallurgy, manufacturing, energy/chemicals, freight, warehousing, defense, and science manufacturing. Shared baseline capabilities keep a business operational; specialist recipes and investment encourage purchasing from others.

Additional divisions currently share umbrella research. Truly independent subsidiaries remain a later question. Starter-kit issuance needs a persistent entitlement record so repeated creation/cancellation does not duplicate equipment.

See [Science](Research%20and%20Science.md), [Defense](Defense%20Industry.md), and [Open Questions](../planning/Open%20Questions.md).

For large-world deployment, see [Cluster and Force Scaling](../architecture/Cluster%20and%20Force%20Scaling.md): a company can have local force bindings on several instances, but each instance has finite force capacity.
