# Defense Industry

**Direction:** specialist corporations manufacture and sell defense equipment for asteroid-exposed shipping and hostile planets. Defense remains relevant in cooperative Play Types.

Possible offers: platform defense packages, planetary perimeter packages, ammunition, installation, repairs, and recurring resupply. Government and corporations can procure these through the exchange. A separate escort ship protecting another platform is not a verified capability.

Research should create saleable improvements: new equipment, ammunition, or supported quality tiers. Ordinary force-wide damage research does not travel with a sold turret; the buyer's operating bonuses and product stats must be distinguishable.

Proposed progression: industry entry → basic products → colored-pack lab research → selected prototype trial → improved manufacturing recipe. Everyone can buy finished defenses, while manufacturing access belongs to the appropriate industry. Common military operating research is explored in [Research and Science](Research%20and%20Science.md).

Open: test objectives, product lineup, fallback public supply, and how prototype cost scales. Fictional product names/stats should stay placeholders until selected dependencies are tested.
