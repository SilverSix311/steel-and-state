# Station Planets and Transit

**Direction:** require Space Age; welcome selected planetary mods. Preferred shared start is a large walkable trade station. Players obtain a corporate opportunity or employment assignment there, then ride government taxis to worksites. Nauvis is one destination.

Proposed districts: arrivals, public employment bureau, corporate registry/recruitment, exchange, warehouse/loading areas, passenger docks, and public industrial services. Keep essential first-use destinations close despite the station's large scale. Player-built station expansions are an appealing proposal, not a required first feature.

Implementation is unresolved: dedicated station surface versus actual platform with walking changes. Test walking, spawn/respawn, remote interactions, civic buildings, transport, and cross-force access. A surface presented as a station may be technically represented as a planet; do not expose implementation jargon in player flows.

Passenger travel could begin with a scripted boarding/travel/arrival service or scheduled physical platforms. Choose after a small experiment. Include return passage, cancellation, disconnect, and missing-dock recovery. New companies should not inherit government research just to travel.

Each supported destination needs a starter package, safe arrival, viable resource/recipe chain, hazard information, and return route. Planet discovery alone is not enough. Concessions probably assign sites rather than whole planets (proposal).

Third-party worlds and artwork are candidates only. See [Dependencies and Sources](../research/Dependencies%20and%20Sources.md), [Trade](Trade%20Warehousing%20and%20Freight.md), and [Roadmap](../planning/Roadmap.md).
