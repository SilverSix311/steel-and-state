# Research and Science

## Current direction

The latest user proposal uses **recipes**, each with Government Science Packs as its only material ingredient, to produce a selected conventional colored science pack. Government packs have no direct lab use. Different output colors have different costs and processing times. All example ratios in conversation were illustrative, not balance targets.

This replaces the earlier assistant recommendation for stock-backed redemption or a centralized conversion queue. Do not carry those recommendations forward as settled rules.

Government trade awards packs for accepted useful deliveries at product-specific rates. Corporate R&D converts them, then labs consume the colored packs required by the selected technology. Milestones and prototype trials support major advances.

The user proposed one lab with each contract and an R&D package that enables more processing. Exact eligible contract types and package contents remain open. The assistant's proposed complete starter setup is one lab, one basic converter, and operating power/supplies. It is not yet an approved bill of materials.

## Three supply paths

| Path | Benefit | Cost |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Government-pack conversion | Industry income funds a modest research stream | Government rewards and slow conversion |
| Purchase colored packs | Use available commercial supply | Price, stock, and transport |
| Science-manufacturing contract | Manufacture from ordinary ingredients and sell surplus | Industry access, production lines, ingredients, power |

Science manufacturing can be added to any corporation. It imports ingredients or adds other contracts to make them. Making packs does not grant all technologies; consuming purchased packs still respects industry prerequisites.

## R&D department sketch

Converter inputs → colored pack storage → labs → selected corporate research.

Keep conversion capacity and lab capacity distinct. More labs cannot fix a shortage of colored packs. Expanded R&D can add equipment or improve processing; exact mechanisms are unchosen. Labs should eventually be obtainable beyond starter contracts (proposal).

General repeatable research can be available independently to all corporations, including military operating improvements. Supplied labs are the leading recommendation; free passive time-based progression and a second parallel research queue are not agreed. Specialist research unlocks product recipes, while buyers can use finished products without learning manufacture.

## Balance questions to test

- A slow recipe can be scaled with more machines. Decide whether equipment cost, recipe cost, power, or a specific capacity rule keeps commercial science competitive. No arbitrary per-company cap has been selected.
- Tune total cost and throughput so ordinary ingredient-based factories remain attractive for sustained research.
- Prevent early bulk resource sales from bypassing all planetary progression; advanced conversion prerequisites remain open.
- Government science issuance is an explicit economic subsidy. Bound useful demand and avoid profitable buy-back/conversion loops.
- The synthesis exception is intentional: the recipe does not consume the ordinary science ingredients. Outputs still become physical stock subject to storage and freight.
- Keep basic research accessible; advanced R&D can be expensive through real supply and infrastructure costs.
- If each contract issues a lab, distinguish recurring jobs from one-time industry starter entitlements before balancing.

## Pseudocode

```text
when a government delivery is verified:
    settle once using the agreed reward schedule
    give the seller its Government Science Packs at the agreed location

when a powered converter runs an eligible colored-pack recipe:
    consume the specified Government Science Packs
    process for the recipe duration
    output actual colored packs

when a corporate lab runs:
    consume the selected technology's required colored packs
    advance that corporation's research

when a major breakthrough has a trial prerequisite:
    verify the objective for the responsible corporation
    unlock eligibility; do not award unrelated technologies
```
