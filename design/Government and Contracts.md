# Government and Contracts

**Direction:** public factories, mining, shipping, warehousing, and defense needs create paid work. Government construction jobs provide the buildings. Output stays in real public warehouses and supports further expansion.

Proposed contract vocabulary:

| Agreement | Purpose | Ownership |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Paid shift | Work at an assigned facility | Employer owns the facility/output |
| Public construction job | Build a functioning public facility | Government owns issued equipment and accepted assets |
| Supply or freight order | Deliver a specified shipment | Ownership changes at the agreed handover |
| Warehouse service | Build, receive, store, or dispatch | Facility owner, stock owner, and operator may differ |
| Industry license | Develop a corporate specialization | Corporation owns its eligible research/production |
| Planetary concession | Develop a specific site | Exact land rights still open |

A public construction job needs machines, power, logistics parts, startup fuel/materials, destination, acceptance terms, and a return route. No missing hand-crafted component should block it. Issue supplies from reserved stock; unused equipment returns to government custody.

Proposed stages: offered → accepted and funded → supplied → in progress → verified → settled. Cancellation and abandoned work need explicit recovery rules. Pay for functioning output/service, not endlessly placing and removing equipment.

Budget bootstrap factories and starting reserves. New facilities replenish stock; deficits create procurement and shipping demand. Keep emergency recovery possible when public stocks or funds run low.

Government trade can award [Government Science Packs](Research%20and%20Science.md). Whether rewards supplement cash, replace it, or offer a choice is open. Corporate deliveries reward the corporate seller; a delivery employee does not automatically own its science.

Government administration may be scripted, admin-run, or eventually elected. This is unresolved and separate from server-admin privileges.
