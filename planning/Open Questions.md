# Open Questions

Keep these open until a concrete example or prototype makes the choice useful. Not a blocker checklist for further brainstorming.

## Next economic choices

- Does the lab entitlement accompany an industry license, an R&D package, or recurring government jobs?
- What does an R&D package add: converter speed, more converters, labs, or recipe access?
- How do conversion cost, power, and machine cost keep commercial science competitive at scale?
- Which government offers pay money, science, or both? Who receives each reward?
- How are advanced colored-pack recipes gated? What does the first prototype trial prove?
- Is common repeatable research supplied-lab progression or passive time-based advancement?

## World and multiplayer

- Expected concurrent players, corporation count, and persistent versus seasonal world?
- Actual platform versus station surface? Physical taxi platform versus scripted trip first?
- Which game version and small planetary mod set should we test?
- Site leasing, resource claims, or another concession arrangement?
- Scripted, administered, or elected government?
- How do offline owners, bankruptcy, absent suppliers, and destroyed starter machinery recover?

## Later depth

- Freight loss, insurance, partial delivery, spoilage, and quality policies?
- Independent subsidiaries, technology licensing, or research outsourcing?
- Detailed PvP enforcement, capture, and offline protection?
- Project license and asset attribution before eventual publication?
