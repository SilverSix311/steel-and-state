## Trade station prompt

```text
Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: wide hero illustration for the Markdown feature prospectus of Steel and State, a planned Factorio: Space Age multiplayer mod. Create a detailed wide landscape image, approximately 2:1, of a huge walkable interplanetary trade station in the visual spirit of Factorio: high-angle isometric industrial game art, weathered steel, rust, concrete panels, exposed pipes, belts, inserter-like cargo manipulators, compact utilitarian factory machines, amber work lights and restrained teal signals. Show a coherent station district: central pedestrian concourse with tiny engineers, an imposing practical government contract office, cargo warehouses full of orderly containers, active conveyor-based loading docks, and a docked rugged cargo spacecraft beyond the platform edge. Space and distant planet visible along the upper edge. Strong readable composition at banner scale, believable mechanical detail, hand-painted pre-rendered strategy-game aesthetic, gritty and lived-in rather than shiny futuristic. No lettering, labels, UI, logos, or watermark. This is original concept art, not an actual game screenshot. Avoid neon cyberpunk, glossy skyscrapers, photoreal people, fantasy architecture.
```

Realistic industrial routing, pipes, cables, crates, tiled floors, hazard stripes, warm amber work lights with restrained colored science highlights, rich readable detail, approximately 2:1 landscape. Hand-painted pre-rendered strategy game appearance. No text, lettering, labels, logos, UI or watermark. Not a screenshot. Avoid sleek sterile laboratories, neon cyberpunk, fantasy magic.
